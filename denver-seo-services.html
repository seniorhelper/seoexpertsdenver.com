Local SEO for Denver businesses

For most local businesses, local search is where results show up first. It covers Google Maps, the local pack and searches that include a place, the moments when someone nearby is ready to call. A strong local presence starts with an accurate, guideline-compliant Google Business Profile and extends to consistent listings across directories, location content with genuine substance, and a steady, honest approach to earning reviews.

  • Google Business Profile review, cleanup and ongoing guidance
  • Citation and name, address and phone consistency checks
  • Location and service-area pages that are genuinely different, not city-name swaps
  • A review request process that follows Google's policies
  • Neighborhood and suburb-level targeting across the metro

Read the Google Business Profile guide, or see what to do if a profile is suspended.

Technical SEO

Technical health decides how well everything else performs. A site with crawl errors, slow pages, a poor mobile experience or broken internal links cannot rank to its potential, however good its content. The expert part is prioritizing: separating the faults that cost traffic from the warnings that do not.

  • Technical review using Google Search Console and crawl tools
  • Crawl and indexing errors found and fixed
  • Core Web Vitals and page speed improvements
  • Mobile-first indexing checks
  • Canonical tags and duplicate content resolution
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt corrections
  • Structured data that accurately describes visible content

See the most common technical mistakes.

On-page SEO

On-page work makes each important page communicate clearly with both searchers and search engines: titles, headings, structure, internal links and, above all, whether the content matches what the searcher wanted. Doing this well means aligning each page with one clear intent, linking related pages so authority flows sensibly, and stating key facts plainly enough for AI features to extract.

  • Title and meta description rewrites for key pages
  • Heading structure that follows the content
  • Internal linking plan and implementation
  • Content gap analysis with page-level recommendations
  • Image alt text and file naming
  • URL and site structure improvements

Content strategy

Content is how a business earns topical authority and gets cited, but only content that answers real questions, shows real expertise and is organized for readers. We plan content around what Denver customers actually search, decide which page should answer each question, and improve or prune what is already there before adding more.

  • Topic and keyword research for your market
  • Content cluster and pillar page planning
  • Service page rewrites and new service pages
  • Educational articles with first-hand detail
  • FAQ content written from real customer questions
  • Content audits: improve, merge or remove

AI search and generative engine optimization

Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini are changing how customers discover businesses. Generative engine optimization is about making your content easy for these systems to understand, trust and cite. For Google, the requirements are the same as for normal search, so the work builds on sound SEO rather than replacing it.

  • Review of how your business appears in AI answers for key questions
  • Content restructuring so key facts are stated clearly and early
  • Entity and business-information consistency across the web
  • Structured data where it accurately reflects the page
  • Depth on the topics you want to be known for

Learn what GEO is or see the AI Overviews diagnosis.

Conversion-focused SEO

Traffic without calls is a vanity metric. Conversion-focused SEO closes the gap between ranking and revenue by examining the whole path from search result to contact: the landing page, the clarity of the next step, trust signals, mobile speed and form friction. Eye To Ad Media has worked conversion-first since its founding, and every recommendation is judged by whether it is likely to produce leads.

  • Landing page review with conversion recommendations
  • Call-to-action placement and wording
  • Mobile experience review
  • Trust elements that are true and verifiable
  • Form and click-to-call improvements
  • Lead tracking from organic search
