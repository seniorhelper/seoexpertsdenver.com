An SEO audit done by an expert, not a tool

Automated audit tools are useful. They are also why so many business owners have a 60-page PDF full of warnings and no idea which three things actually matter. An expert audit uses the same tools, then does the part they cannot: deciding what is wrong, what is noise, and what to do first.

Quick answer: what is included in an expert SEO audit?

An expert SEO audit reviews how Google crawls and indexes your site, how well your key pages match what customers search for, your local presence and Google Business Profile, your content against the competitors who outrank you, and your conversion path, using your real Search Console and analytics data. The result is a short, prioritized plan written by a person, not a list of every warning a tool can generate.

A tool report versus an expert audit

Crawlers flag thousands of issues on almost any site: missing alt text, long titles, redirect hops, duplicate meta descriptions. Most of those flags are real but trivial. A few are critical. The tool cannot tell which, because it does not know your business, your market, or what changed last month.

What the expert audit reviews

Crawling and indexing

Which pages Google has indexed and which it has skipped, and why. The Page indexing report, robots.txt, canonical tags, noindex directives, sitemap accuracy, redirect chains, server errors and how the pages render for Googlebot. This is where the sudden, site-wide problems live.

Search demand and page targeting

The searches that bring customers to businesses like yours in Denver, matched against the pages you have. Often the gaps are obvious once laid out: a service with no page, two pages competing for the same search, or a page targeting a term nobody uses.

Content quality against the competition

A page-by-page read of your most important pages beside the ones that outrank them. Does yours answer the real questions? Does it show first-hand experience? Is the key information easy to find? This is the part no tool can do.

Local presence

Your Google Business Profile against Google's guidelines, categories, service areas, reviews and responses, and whether your name, address and phone match across your site and major listings. For multi-location businesses, whether each location page has genuinely local substance.

AI search readiness

Whether your key pages are eligible for Google's AI features, whether the facts you want repeated are stated clearly, and how your business is described in the places answer engines draw on.

Conversion path

Traffic that does not turn into calls is wasted. We check mobile speed and layout, calls to action, forms, phone links and tracking on the pages that receive search traffic. Eye To Ad Media's approach has always been conversion-first, and this audit is no exception.

What you receive

  • A written summary in plain English: what is working, what is broken, and what matters most.
  • A prioritized action list grouped into fix now, fix next and leave alone, with the reason for each.
  • Specific instructions your developer or staff can follow, or a fixed quote if you want us to do the work.
  • A walkthrough call to go through the findings and answer questions.
  • A baseline of current clicks, positions and conversions, so any future work can be measured fairly.

You keep the findings whether or not you hire us for anything else.

The free first look and the full audit

The free first look is a short review of the public site and your search presence, enough to tell you whether there is a serious problem and what kind. It carries no contract. The full expert audit goes much deeper and needs read-only access to Search Console and analytics. It is quoted as a fixed fee, based on the size of the site and how much data there is to review, before any work begins.

How to prepare for an audit

  1. Add read-only access for Search Console and analytics. If you have never set up Search Console, that becomes finding number one.
  2. List the services and areas that matter most to the business, and the ones you want to grow.
  3. Note any big changes in the last two years: redesigns, platform moves, new domains, previous SEO providers.
  4. Name three competitors you lose business to, not just the ones you know rank well.

That is all. There is no long intake form and nothing to buy in advance.

Questions people ask

How is an expert SEO audit different from a free online SEO checker?

A checker crawls your site and scores it against generic rules. An expert audit combines that crawl with your actual Search Console and analytics data, your competitors and your business goals, then decides which few issues matter and in what order.

How long does an expert SEO audit take?

It depends on the size of the site and how much data there is to review. We give you the timeline along with the fixed quote before work begins.

Do I have to hire you to fix what the audit finds?

No. The findings are yours and are written so a developer or staff member can act on them. If you want us to do the work, it is quoted separately as a fixed price.

What access do you need?

Read-only access to Google Search Console and analytics is enough for most audits. We do not need your website login or hosting credentials for the review itself.

Is the free first look really free?

Yes. It is a short review of your public site and search presence with no contract and no obligation. It tells you whether a deeper audit is worth doing.
