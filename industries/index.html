Why industry context matters in SEO

Google's ranking systems apply the same principles to every site, but what counts as helpful depends on the subject and the searcher. Google's quality rater guidelines, for example, hold pages on health, finance and safety to a higher standard of trust than pages on hobbies. Regulators add their own rules for some sectors, such as financial advisers' use of testimonials or healthcare providers' use of tracking tools. And demand behaves differently: a ski rental shop and a tax preparer both have seasons, at opposite ends of the year.

An expert who understands those differences avoids two expensive mistakes. The first is applying a tactic that is normal in one industry and risky in another, like soliciting reviews in a profession whose ethics codes discourage it. The second is misreading the data, like treating a predictable seasonal dip as a ranking crisis.
