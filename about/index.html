About SEO Experts Denver

SEO Experts Denver is the one-to-one, expert-led side of Eye To Ad Media, a Denver marketing company founded by Zach Wennstedt in 2012. It exists for businesses that need someone experienced to look at a specific problem, rather than a package.

Built by a business owner who got burned

In 2012 Zach Wennstedt was running a bath remodeling business in Denver and needed customers. Over the next several years he paid web developers who took the money and disappeared, and he paid highly rated Denver agencies whose work, often done by junior staff, did not make his phone ring. By his own account the wasted spend came to somewhere between $30,000 and $40,000, at a time when he could not afford to lose it.

Out of necessity he became his own marketer. He studied conversion optimization, SEO and digital advertising with the focus of someone whose livelihood depended on the result, because it did. That hard-won expertise became Eye To Ad Media, which has served businesses across Denver and Colorado since 2012. The full story is on the Eye To Ad Media about page.

The experience shaped one conviction that runs through everything on this site: rankings that do not produce calls are a failure, not a success. A visitor who cannot find the phone number, a form that silently breaks, a page that ranks for the wrong search, all of it wastes the visibility you worked for.

Why a separate expert site?

Some businesses need an ongoing marketing program. Many do not. They need someone to find out why traffic fell, to check a migration plan before launch, to get a Business Profile reinstated, or to coach the person who already runs their website. Those jobs call for expert judgment applied to one problem, priced as a defined project, and that is what SEO Experts Denver is for.

The person you talk to matters. Zach works directly on client accounts, and the client roster is kept deliberately small so that stays true. You will not be handed from a salesperson to an account manager to someone you never meet.

How we work

Diagnosis first

We look at your data before recommending anything. Most expensive SEO mistakes start with fixing the wrong problem.

Conversion first

Every recommendation is judged by whether it is likely to produce calls, forms and customers.

Plain language

You get explanations you can follow and repeat to your team, not jargon and dashboards.

Fixed quotes

Project work is priced as one written number before it starts. The first look is free with no contract.

A saying Zach's father passed on to him sums up the attitude: businesses that do business like businesses of yesterday will not be doing business tomorrow. Search is changing fast, with AI answers above the results and new ways for customers to find you, and the work has to change with it.

Rules this site follows

  • No invented results. No made-up statistics, case studies, testimonials, reviews or ratings appear on this site.
  • No guaranteed rankings. Google warns against anyone who promises them, and so do we.
  • Sourced claims. Facts about how Google works link to Google's own documentation. Market prices come from a cited survey, not our imagination.
  • You own your accounts. Search Console, analytics, Business Profile, domain and content stay in your name.
  • Honest referrals. If paid ads, a different provider or doing nothing would serve you better, we will say so.
