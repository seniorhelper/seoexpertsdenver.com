What a real SEO expert does

Expertise means knowing which of a thousand tasks matters this month

There are hundreds of things you could change on a website. Most of them will not move anything. The value of an experienced SEO consultant is judgment: reading the evidence, ruling things out, and putting the few changes that matter in the right order.

Effective SEO starts with your market: what your customers type, what the businesses outranking you do well, and what is holding your site back technically. From there it becomes disciplined work: content that answers real questions, signals of trust that are earned rather than bought, and a site structured so search engines and AI systems can read it with confidence. Our approach combines proven SEO fundamentals with practical generative engine optimization.

  • Expert audit by hand. Crawl data, Search Console, analytics and the live pages, read by a person, not dumped from a tool.
  • Search intent research. The exact words Denver customers use and what they expect to find when they click.
  • On-page and content fixes. Rewrite or restructure the pages that should rank, and prune the ones dragging the site down.
  • Local signals and Business Profile. Map pack visibility across the Denver metro, and help when a profile gets suspended.
  • AI search readiness. Clear, citable content that Google's AI features and other answer engines can understand.

Questions about hiring a Denver SEO expert

What does an SEO expert actually do?

An SEO expert researches your market, audits your website, and works out what is holding your rankings back. That includes fixing technical problems that stop search engines from crawling the site, improving the pages that should answer your customers' questions, strengthening local signals such as your Google Business Profile, and diagnosing specific events like traffic drops, Google updates or migrations. Good experts explain the reasoning, so you understand what changed and why.

How long does SEO take to show results in Denver?

It depends on what is wrong. Fixing a clear technical fault, such as pages accidentally blocked from indexing, can show up within weeks once Google recrawls. Building rankings in a competitive market takes longer: Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says that in most cases it takes four months to a year from the start of changes before you see the benefit. Anyone who promises a fixed date is guessing.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in Google's regular results. Generative engine optimization (GEO) focuses on being cited in AI-generated answers from tools like Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. They overlap heavily: Google says its AI features draw on pages that are already indexed and eligible to show a snippet, so sound SEO is the foundation for both.

How much does an SEO expert cost in Denver?

It varies with the scope. In Ahrefs' poll of 439 SEO providers, the most common hourly band was $75 to $100 and the most common monthly retainer band was $501 to $1,000, with many providers above and below those bands. Expert audits and repair projects are usually priced as a fixed fee. We quote project work as a fixed written number before anything starts.

Should I choose SEO over paid ads?

Not necessarily over, but alongside. Paid ads deliver visibility quickly and stop the moment the budget stops. SEO builds visibility that keeps working, but it takes longer. Many Denver businesses run both early on and shift weight toward organic search as it grows. If your problem is urgent and short-term, an honest expert will tell you that ads may be the better tool.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. One-to-one consulting suits small and mid-sized businesses well, because you pay for expert judgment on your specific problem rather than a large package. Many small businesses only need a diagnosis and a clear plan their own staff or web person can follow.

Does my business need a Google Business Profile?

Your Google Business Profile is the listing that appears in Google Maps and the local pack when people search for your business or your type of service nearby. Keeping categories, services, hours, photos and reviews accurate affects how often it appears. For most local businesses it is one of the most valuable things to get right, and if it is suspended, fixing that comes before anything else.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and AI answers?

We can improve the odds, but no one can guarantee inclusion. For Google's AI features, the requirements are the same as for normal search: indexable pages that are eligible for a snippet. Beyond that, clear, specific, well-sourced content and consistent information about your business across the web make it easier for any answer engine to understand and cite you.
